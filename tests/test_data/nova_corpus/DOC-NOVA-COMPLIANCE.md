# Regulatory Compliance Policy: EU Cross-Border Transactions

**Governance Body:** European Financial Security Authority (EFSA) Compliance  
**Document Code:** REG-SEC-2026-EU  
**Effective Date:** October 1, 2026  

## Section 4: Mandatory Transport Security
All electronic fund transfers and checkout transactions originating in the European Economic Area (EEA) must be transported over end-to-end encrypted tunnels utilizing mutual TLS 1.3 or higher.

## Penalties for Non-Compliance
1. **Tier-1 Breach:** Any service that drops transactional audit logs or operates customer payment workflows over unauthenticated or unencrypted cache channels will trigger an immediate **Tier-1 Regulatory Breach**.
2. **Consequences:** Triggers an automatic mandatory suspension of the operator's EU merchant processing license and statutory fines of €20,000,000 or 4% of global annual turnover.
